Money
Euro (Republic): Dublin Airport, Maldron, Rosapenna and all three of its courses, Ballyliffin, Jameson Links, Royal Dublin, the Davenport.

Sterling (Northern Ireland): The Ebrington, Portstewart, Royal Portrush.

Ballyliffin sits further north than any point in Northern Ireland and is still in the Republic — euro. Paying in the wrong currency at border businesses gets you a poor rate. Northern Irish banknotes are sterling but will not be taken in the Republic, so spend them before Sat 26.
Phone & roaming
The Republic and Northern Ireland are separate roaming zones since Brexit, and most Canadian travel passes price Ireland and the UK separately. Make sure the plan or eSIM lists both.

Inadvertent roaming is a real problem on this route — Derry, Inishowen and the Downings–Derry run all latch onto the wrong side’s network. Turn off automatic network selection near the border, or pin the network by hand, and watch for the carrier-change text.

112 works on both sides of the border; 999 also works in Northern Ireland.
Before you tee off
  • Royal Portrush: recognised club membership required and a handicap certificate may be requested. No published handicap limit. Dress code enforced — no blue denim, collarless shirts or cargo/beach shorts. Office +44 28 7082 2311.
  • St Patrick’s: walking only, no buggies, no shuttle from the hotel.
  • Buggies: several Irish links require a medical certificate before releasing one. Confirm with each club in advance, not on the first tee.
  • Laundry: The Ebrington, Wed–Fri. It is the midpoint of the trip.
